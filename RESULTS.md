# Results

Generated by `scripts/run_analysis.py` from the official, hash checked metadata tables. All numbers are aggregates. Rules: [ANALYSIS_PLAN.md](ANALYSIS_PLAN.md). Task caveat first: this is a yoga tree one-leg stance with arms overhead inside cued 4 s windows, not the clinical one-leg stand test, and the sample is healthy adults only.

## 2. Primary sample

all short captures (MNTRL, MNTRR) of all 32 participants, attempts grouped into cued 4 s windows (new window when the foot lifts at least 2.0 s after the previous attempt ended); one summary per person over all their short windows; Old minus Young.

People: 15 Young, 17 Old. Windows: 267 Young, 362 Old. Attempts per window, mean: Young 1.02, Old 1.41 (max 2 and 5).
